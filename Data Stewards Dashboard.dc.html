Runbook — single-feed stalls

Owner: Data Infrastructure · updated 14 Aug 2026 · reviewed quarterly

A feed is stalled when its most recent modified timestamp has not advanced across consecutive daily snapshots while the endpoint continues to return 200. Stalls are the most common publisher-side failure and are almost always caused by an upstream export job, not by the feed itself.

Detection

The nightly job compares max(modified) per feed against the previous snapshot. A feed with no advance for 2+ snapshots opens an incident; the incident carries its own age so the {{ thresholdDays }}-day contact threshold is measured from first detection, not from the last run.

{{ codeSample }}

Triage sequence

  1. Confirm the stall is not a genuine seasonal pause — check whether future opportunity count is also falling.
  2. Check the dataset-wide stall monitor. If every feed for the publisher is stalled, treat it as one incident, not several.
  3. Re-run the feed fetch manually and compare the raw payload against the stored snapshot.
  4. At day {{ thresholdDays }}, the incident enters the contact queue. Use the draft email action and log the contact date.
Do not chase a stall inside the first 48 hours. Roughly a third of them self-resolve when the publisher's next scheduled export runs.
